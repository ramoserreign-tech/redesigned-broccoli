Your response matters

Emergency guide

Simple steps to help you stay informed and follow the school’s response before, during, and after an emergency.

01

Before an emergency

Be ready before it happens.

  • Know the school’s evacuation areas and routes.
  • Listen to announcements from the school SDRRM organization.
  • Keep emergency supplies accessible.
  • Know and remember your emergency contacts.
  • Participate in school drills and preparedness activities.
  • Follow official weather and disaster advisories.
02

During an emergency

Stay calm. Listen. Follow instructions.

  • Stay calm, alert, and prepared.
  • Listen attentively to teachers, school personnel, and SDRRM officers.
  • Do not panic or run. Evacuate when instructed and when it is safe to do so.
03

After an emergency

Wait for the all-clear.

  • Wait for official instructions, announcements, or a signal before returning to affected areas.
  • Check yourself and others for injuries and tell a teacher or responder if someone needs help.
  • Stay away from damaged structures and dangerous areas.
  • Report hazards to teachers or SDRRM officers.
  • Follow school instructions about the resumption of classes.
✳
In immediate danger?

Follow your teacher’s or SDRRM personnel’s instructions and contact the appropriate emergency service.

Disaster safety guide · 01

Typhoon safety

Stay informed, secure what you can, and follow school and official weather advisories.

Before

Get prepared.

  • Monitor official weather advisories or news.
  • Secure personal belongings.
  • Prepare emergency supplies.
  • Know your school’s evacuation routes.
  • Follow school announcements.
During

Stay in a safe place.

  • Stay indoors and away from windows.
  • Follow instructions and announcements from school authorities.
  • Do not go outside during dangerous conditions.
After

Watch for hazards.

  • Avoid floodwater and damaged electrical wires or areas.
  • Wait for official announcements.
  • Report hazards or damage to school personnel.

While the ground is shaking

  • Stay away from windows, especially glass windows, and objects that might fall.
  • Protect your head and neck while taking cover.
  • Follow the school’s evacuation procedures after the shaking stops.
  • Wait for instructions from teachers and SDRRM officers.

Disaster safety guide · 04

Flood safety

Floodwater can hide hazards. Keep away and move to a designated safe area when instructed.

Before

Plan ahead.

  • Monitor weather and flood advisories.
  • Keep important belongings in safe locations.
  • Know the school’s evacuation routes.
During

Move to safety.

  • Move to the designated safe area when instructed.
  • Avoid entering floodwater.
  • Stay away from electrical equipment or exposed wires.
After

Wait for clearance.

  • Do not enter damaged buildings until teachers, school personnel, and SDRRM officers declare them safe.
  • Follow school announcements and report hazards.

About the DRRM organization

Preparedness is a shared responsibility.

The School Disaster Risk Reduction and Management (SDRRM) Organization helps promote disaster preparedness, risk reduction, response, and recovery within the school community. It supports activities that help students, teachers, and school personnel understand disaster risks and respond appropriately during emergencies.

01 · Prepare

Preparedness

Preparing people, plans, resources, and procedures before disasters.

02 · Reduce risk

Prevention & mitigation

Reducing disaster risks and minimizing possible impacts.

03 · Act

Response

Taking appropriate actions during and immediately after an emergency.

04 · Restore

Recovery

Supporting the restoration of school activities and conditions after a disaster.

What is DRRM?

Disaster Risk Reduction and Management

DRRM refers to actions and strategies that help reduce disaster risks, prepare people for emergencies, respond effectively when disasters occur, and support recovery afterward.
